# Avana

**Avana** is a lending protocol built on Aave V4 that enables AMM liquidity pool positions to be used as collateral. Liquidity providers from Uniswap, Balancer, Curve, or Aerodrome can deposit supported LP positions, have them evaluated inside market-specific risk frameworks, and borrow against them while the positions stay active in the underlying pools.

Instead of forcing liquidity providers to withdraw, sell, or unwind their positions, Avana unlocks borrowing power directly from AMM liquidity. LPs can keep earning fees while accessing liquidity when they need it. Users Provide liquidity. Use it as collateral. Borrow without unwinding your position.

Avana competitor is [Fluid](https://fluid.instadapp.io/) (Instadapp).. Fluid basically built its own unified liquidity layer, where collateral and debt can also be used as DEX liquidity inside one vertically integrated system.

Avana takes a different approach. Instead of owning the liquidity rails, Avana plugs into the AMMs that already exist and lets users borrow against LP positions from across different Dexes.

So the big difference is pretty simple: Fluid builds and controls the liquidity infrastructure, while Avana aggregates liquidity positions from the broader DeFi ecosystem and turns them into usable collateral.


## Why Avana?

Liquidity providers should not have to choose between earning trading fees and accessing capital. With Avana, LPs can remain active in liquidity markets while unlocking borrowing power from their positions. This creates a more efficient DeFi experience where liquidity is not trapped, idle, or difficult to use.

Avana is building a new credit layer for DeFi, one where LP positions become a core collateral primitive for borrowing and capital efficiency.
